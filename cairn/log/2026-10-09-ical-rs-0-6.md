# ical-rs 0.6, and the workarounds it retires

## What landed

- ical-rs 0.6, io-gcal 0.2, io-msgraph 0.5 (both on ical-rs 0.6) and io-pimdir 0.7 from crates.io in place of the git revision `be03184`; `Cargo.lock` holds one ical-rs and one io-pimdir.
- `is_spurious` removed: ical-rs 0.6 takes a `DATE` and a `TZID` on the time properties and the `ORGANIZER` parameters, so `check` reports every finding. It now also reports a duration outside RFC 5545 3.3.6 and a `LANGUAGE` or `ALTREP` the RFC does not give a property.
- `folded` removed: the encoder folds a built line at 75 octets, never inside a UTF-8 sequence, so a flag's line goes in as `IcalItem::Prop` directly.
- `--duration` checked through ical-rs's validator (`IcalValidateError::Duration`); the hand-written grammar is gone. Same message; `PT1H5S` (a second straight after an hour), which the old check let through, is now refused. A negative duration stays refused by calendula.
- Expansion (`src/shared/event/expand.rs`) on ical-rs 0.6's zone-aware set: `of_component_in` and `with_override_in` with one `IcalTz` per `TZID` the series names, resolved as calendula reads it (the database zone described around the series' start, else the calendar's `VTIMEZONE`, else the local zone); `localize_until` removed. A UTC `EXDATE` now drops its instance of a zoned series, and a UTC `RECURRENCE-ID` overrides it rather than listing beside it.
- The four broken intra-doc links fixed, `cargo doc` clean under `-D warnings`.
- Capability moved: `commands` (the composed-item check, the field flags' folding and `--duration`, recurrence expansion across clocks).

`cargo test --all-features` and `cargo test` (179 tests each), `cargo clippy --all-features --all-targets -D warnings` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -D warnings` clean, `cargo fmt --check`, `RUSTDOCFLAGS="-D warnings" cargo doc --no-deps`, all features and default, and builds of each backend alone with `rustls-ring`, `pimdir` and `vdir` alone, no default features, `native-tls` with `vendored`, and `rustls-aws` with the rest.
