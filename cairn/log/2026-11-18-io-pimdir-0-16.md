# io-pimdir 0.16, a search paged by cursor

Landed without a proposal: a dependency bump, io-pimdir 0.15.1 to 0.16.0, which carries PIM-1's checked arithmetic and moves search paging to a cursor (TRI-9).

- **CAL-3 closes.** The 19-line hostile VTIMEZONE of the analysis (repro/verify/pimpanic/hostile.ics) given to `item add` on a draft-13 store seeded with sqlite3 aborted the 0.15.1 binary ("attempt to multiply with overflow", exit 101); the 0.16.0 one queues it, `{"id":"hostile-1","queued":true}`, exit 0.
- `pimdir search` pages by `PimdirSearchCursor`: `nextPage` is the cursor's token, `--page-token` takes it back, and `--page <n>` walks the cursors of the pages before it as cardamum does, a page past the last answering no hit. The text keeps `Next page: --page <n+1>` after a numbered page, the cursor after a token. tests/pimdir_search.rs follows (`a_search_resumes_at_the_next_page_it_names`, `an_unreadable_page_token_is_refused`).

Capabilities moved: commands.
